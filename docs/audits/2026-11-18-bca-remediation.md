## Invariants and negative cases

- Preserve immediate check-in delivery. The fixed hotel catch-up sweeps remain 12:00 and 21:00 in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`; no tenant-defined time.
- A safe-unsent record newly eligible at 21:03 schedules the next strictly future boundary, 21:10. At exactly 21:10 it schedules 21:20. Calculate at actual failure/recovery time, not request start. Recovery after 21:10 may therefore schedule 21:20.
- Only actual same-current-Vietnam-day arrivals still in-house are eligible for automatic catch-up. Do not fabricate arrival dates or roll a prior-day stay into today. Recheck immediately before dispatch and after midnight.
- Separate data rejection, safe-unsent transient failure, invalid authentication and UNKNOWN transmission. Safe read retries wait 10 seconds between attempts, up to two further attempts; exhausted recovery pauses for 60 seconds with bounded/coalesced probes. Preserve valid login and auto-send intent during transient disconnect.
- Never blindly repeat a timed-out/ambiguous POST. Reconcile first; incomplete/failed/partial reads never prove absence. Never resend a confirmed submitted guest. Do not convert all 400/401/403/404/429/5xx responses into one category without endpoint/result context.
- Auto-send off, explicit logout, session/account changes and restart must fence queued work and late responses. No hidden password login or real BCA operation during validation. Local guest lists remain usable while BCA is unavailable.
- Hotel/tenant/room authorization and authoritative DB filtering apply to every entry point. Bind guest data, provider origin, BCA facility and connection/session generation at selection, dispatch and result persistence.
- A BCA facility must not silently receive lists from two different VietSage hotels/tenants. Default to exclusive `(normalized provider origin, CsltId)` ownership across hotels, including different tenants. Reject conflicting links/dispatches safely and atomically; do not disclose another tenant's identity or automatically reassign/delete its configuration. Do not infer facility ownership from username alone. Existing conflicting links must fail closed with an actionable local state.
- No request, response-body read, cancellation or cleanup waits forever. Recovery/probe delays do not block browser/BFF requests; polling local status must not itself trigger provider calls. No new broker/infrastructure by default.
